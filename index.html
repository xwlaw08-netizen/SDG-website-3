About CastOFF

MAKE SPOTTING NETS EASIER

What is CastOFF?

CastOFF connect users to conservationists by sending pictures of the ghost net to the conservationists for it to be cleaned up. This allows the ocean to be cleaned faster and minimize risks before it get worse

Why do we do this?

According to statistics, 500,000 to 1 million tons of abandoned, lost, or discarded fishing gear (ghost nets) enter the world's oceans each year. That is why I made this app to make the oceans healthy again.
